## Design & Branding Guidelines

### Suggested Color Palette:
- **Primary**: Deep navy / dark blue (ثقة واحترافية)
- **Accent**: Gold or warm amber (ثقافة وأصالة)
- **Background**: Off-white / light cream
- **Text**: Dark charcoal (#1a1a2e or similar)

### Typography:
- Use a modern Arabic font (e.g., IBM Plex Arabic, Tajawal, Cairo, or Almarai from Google Fonts)
- Headings: Bold, large
- Body: Regular, readable size (16-18px)

### Layout:
- Full-width sections with alternating backgrounds
- Generous whitespace
- Mobile-first responsive design
- RTL throughout

### Imagery Suggestions:
- Cultural events, Saudi cultural landmarks
- Professional workshops and training environments
- Abstract geometric patterns inspired by Islamic art (for decorative elements)

---

## Technical Notes for Implementation

1. **RTL Layout**: Ensure `<html dir="rtl" lang="ar">` is set
2. **Font Loading**: Load Arabic Google Font in `<head>`
3. **Smooth Scrolling**: Primary CTA should smooth-scroll to registration form
4. **Responsive**: Must work on mobile (majority of Saudi traffic is mobile)
5. **Form Handling**: Registration form can use a simple POST endpoint or integrate with a service (Google Forms, Typeform, etc.)
6. **Animation**: Subtle scroll-reveal animations for sections
7. **Accessibility**: Proper heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3), alt text on images, contrast ratios
